BTI 4 Intern Performance & Recognition

Grades document recorded contributions and available evidence for transparent recognition. They are not a ranking, and recorded counts are not a complete measure of anyone’s overall value; coordination, moderation and leadership are valued too.

Grade distribution

Grading scale

O — Outstanding
Exceptional contribution, strong output, consistency, initiative, or significant impact supported by available evidence.
A — Good
Good contribution, meaningful participation, or satisfactory and consistent delivery.
B — Average / Below Average
Limited recorded contribution, low activity, inconsistent delivery, or insufficient evidence of broader contribution during the assessment period.
